HabitCopilot

Build habits where they belong — with a coach that runs entirely on your iPhone.

HabitCopilot helps you organize habits into the routines of your day, check them off, and get short, grounded nudges when a streak is about to slip. There is no account to create and no sign-in. What you write stays on your device.

Private by design On-device AI No account No ads No cross-app tracking

What it does

  • Group habits into time-of-day routines (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and your own).
  • One-tap check-ins, with streaks tracked locally.
  • An AI coach that runs on your device, offering brief, fact-grounded encouragement and gentle nudges. On iPhones without on-device AI, HabitCopilot falls back to non-AI coaching templates.
  • Optional local reminders before a streak breaks and at the times you choose.
  • Insights into how your routines are going, computed on your device.

Your privacy comes first

All coaching runs on your device, so what you write — your habits, notes, and coach conversations — never leaves it. There is no account, no advertising, and no cross-app tracking. HabitCopilot does collect anonymous usage analytics — event counts such as “a check-in happened,” never the content of your habits, notes, or chats — to understand how the app is used.
