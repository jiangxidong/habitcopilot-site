HabitCopilot

Build habits where they belong — with a coach that runs entirely on your iPhone.

HabitCopilot helps you organize habits into the routines of your day, check them off, and get short, grounded nudges when a streak is about to slip. There is no account to create and no sign-in. What you write stays on your device.

Private by design On-device AI No account No ads No cross-app tracking

What it does

  • Group habits into time-of-day routines (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and your own).
  • One-tap check-ins, with streaks tracked locally.
  • A coach that runs on your device and speaks up first, with short cards — Alerts, Insights, Suggestions, and Wins — each backed by a fact from your own check-ins. On iPhones without on-device AI, HabitCopilot uses template wording and every card still shows up.
  • A Weekly review in the Coach tab, plus one-tap plan changes you can undo.
  • Gentle, Steady, or Firm: choose how direct the coach gets.
  • A Journey for every habit, and 12 weeks of check-ins at a glance.
  • Widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen: today's habits at a glance.
  • Optional local reminders at the times you choose.

Your privacy comes first

All coaching runs on your device, so your habits and check-ins never leave it. There is no account, no advertising, and no cross-app tracking. HabitCopilot does collect anonymous usage analytics — event counts such as “a check-in happened,” never the content of your habits or anything you write — to understand how the app is used.
