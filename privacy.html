Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

HabitCopilot is built to be private by default. We do not collect any data. Everything you create in the app — your habits, routines, check-ins, notes, and coach history — is stored only on your device. This policy explains what that means in plain terms.

Data we collect

None. HabitCopilot has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. We do not ask for your name, email, or any personal information to use the app, and we have no servers that receive your data.

Where your data lives

All of your app data is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device database (SwiftData). It is not transmitted off the device by HabitCopilot. If you have iCloud backups enabled for your device, your local app data may be included in your own encrypted iCloud backup, which is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy — not by us.

On-device AI

The coaching features use Apple's on-device Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence). Your habit data and anything you type to the coach are processed on your device to generate responses. This content is not sent to HabitCopilot or to any third party, and it is not used to train any model.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, HabitCopilot schedules local notifications on your device. Because a reminder is about a specific habit, the notification text may include the name of that habit (for example, a streak reminder for a habit you named). These notifications are created and delivered locally by iOS; their content is not sent to us.

Third parties

HabitCopilot includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and does not share data with anyone, because there is no data to share.

Deleting your data

You are always in control of your data:

  • Delete an individual habit or routine at any time from within the app.
  • Deleting the HabitCopilot app from your iPhone permanently removes all of its data from your device.

Children's privacy

HabitCopilot is a general-audience habit tracker and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date above. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
